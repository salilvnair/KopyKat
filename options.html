Welcome to KopyKat

Get set up in three quick steps — no app‑code changes.

  1. 1
    Add your keys The sessionStorage keys to mirror, e.g. currentUser.
  2. 2
    Set the gate & direction Allow‑list your origins, then pick which are From (copy) and which are To (paste).
  3. 3
    Turn on the engine Flip Sync engine on and hit Save. Reload your tabs and you're syncing.

Auto-reload synced tabs on update

Reloads allow-listed tabs after the extension is reinstalled/updated so their content scripts re-attach. Reloads discard unsaved page state.

Keyboard shortcut

Toggle the sync engine with Ctrl+Shift+Y (⌘+Shift+Y on Mac). Rebind at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
