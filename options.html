Welcome to KopyKat! Three steps: 1) add the sessionStorage keys to mirror, 2) add the origins to sync, 3) flip the sync engine on. That's it — no app code changes.
Sync engine

Stays fully inactive until enabled and origins are allow-listed.

Auto-reload synced tabs on update

Reloads allow-listed tabs after the extension is reinstalled/updated so their content scripts re-attach. Reloads discard unsaved page state.

Keyboard shortcut

Toggle the sync engine with Ctrl+Shift+Y (⌘+Shift+Y on Mac). Rebind at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
